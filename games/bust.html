      How to play Bust!

      Goal

      Win by playing every card in your hand, or by being the last player remaining.

      Setup

      • A standard 52-card deck plus two Jokers (54 cards). 2–6 players; each is dealt 7 cards. The rest form the draw pile.
      • The top card of the draw pile starts the play pile and sets the count. It must be a number card (A–10). Face cards and Jokers are shuffled back and another is flipped. It always counts as positive.
      • Play starts clockwise.

      Your turn

      • Play one card from your hand, or play the top card of the draw pile blind — its effect applies no matter what it is.
      • If the count goes below 0 or above 20, you bust and are eliminated. 0 and 20 are safe.
      • Playing your last card wins instantly — unless that card busts you.

      Cards

      Red 2–10Add the value to the count.
      Black 2–10Subtract the value from the count.
      AcesWorth 1 (red +1, black −1) and reverse the direction of play.
      Red QueenCount becomes 20.
      Black QueenCount becomes 0.
      JacksThe next player's turn is skipped, and they draw 1 card.
      JokersThe next player must play the top card of the draw pile, blind. Jokers have no color.
      Red KingThe next player must play a red card from their hand.
      Black KingThe next player must play a black card from their hand.

      Kings in detail

      • A player without the required color has their turn skipped, and the requirement passes on until someone plays that color.
      • While a color is required, you can't play from the draw pile, and you can't play a Joker (Jokers have no color).
      • If the requirement travels all the way back to the King's player, that player is eliminated.

      Busts & eliminations

      • An eliminated player's hand goes to the bottom of the play pile.
      • After a bust, a new card is flipped from the draw pile (like the start of the game) to set a new count.
      • When the draw pile runs out, the play pile (except its top card) is shuffled into a new draw pile.
