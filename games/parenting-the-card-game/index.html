Parenting: the Card Game

Players

How to Play

Goal: Keep your toddler's needs met. Three strikes and you're out. The last parent standing wins!

Care Levels

Every toddler has six care levels. Each starts at 4 and stays between 1 and 6. The number beside each symbol on a toddler card is that level, and it turns red at 2 or below.

{0} Hunger   {1} Health   {2} Bladder   {3} Sleep   {4} Learning   {5} Happiness

Your Turn

  1. Roll: Three dice roll for you. Each number lowers its care level by 1:
    1 = {0}   2 = {1}   3 = {2}   4 = {3}   5 = {4}   6 = {5}
  2. Triples: If all three dice match, that care level drops straight to 1.
  3. Crisis: Any care level at 1 is in crisis (see below).
  4. Play a Card: Click one of your glowing +2 cards to add 2 to that care level (up to 6).
  5. Draw: Your hand refills to 5 cards, and the table turns to the next player.

Parenting Support Cards

  • +2 Cards show +2 and a care symbol, like +2 {0}. Play one each turn.
  • Rescue Cards show a life preserver and a care symbol, like {6}{0}. They are only used during a crisis.

Toddler in Crisis

  • If you hold the matching Rescue Card {6} for every care level in crisis, they are played for you and each of those levels goes up 3.
  • If not, you take a strike, each level in crisis goes up 2, and you keep your Rescue Cards for later.
  • You can only get one strike per turn.
